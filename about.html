MY STORY

The road is where I learn the most.

My love for exploring places grew through visits to forts, historic sites and lesser-known destinations. What started as weekends of discovering new routes became a habit of documenting the journey so others could visit with fewer surprises.

I travel solo on my motorcycle whenever I can. Some trips lead to waterfalls in Telangana; others take me to forts around Pune, temple towns or quiet villages that rarely appear in the usual travel lists.

I want every guide here to answer practical questions first: how to reach, when to go, what to pay, what to carry and what to be careful about. The personal journey comes next, so you get both the useful information and the story behind it.

A good travel guide should make the journey feel possible, not complicated.
